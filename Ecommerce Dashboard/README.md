
# 🛒 E-commerce Sales Dashboard

## 📝 Project Overview
This project presents an **E-commerce Sales Dashboard** built in Power BI to analyze customer orders, profitability, and regional performance.  
The dashboard enables stakeholders to track **sales, profit, order quantities, and customer behavior** with an interactive view.

---

## 🔍 Business Questions Answered

### Sales & Profit Analysis
- What is the **overall sales amount, profit, quantity sold, and average order value (AOV)**?
- How do **profits vary by month**? Which months drive the highest/lowest profit?
- Which **states contribute the most revenue**?
- Who are the **top customers by sales amount**?

### Category & Sub-Category Insights
- What is the **distribution of sales quantities across categories** (Clothing, Electronics, Furniture)?
- Which **sub-categories generate the highest profit**?
- Are there any sub-categories dragging down profitability?

### Payment & Customer Behavior
- What is the **preferred payment mode** (COD, UPI, Cards, EMI, etc.)?
- Which **customers contribute the most to sales**?
- How does **customer purchasing behavior** differ across regions?

---

## 💡 How This Helped the Company
- 📍 **Regional strategy** – Identified top-performing states like **Maharashtra** and **Madhya Pradesh** for targeted marketing.  
- 👗 **Category focus** – Clothing accounted for **62% of total quantity**, guiding inventory stocking decisions.  
- 💳 **Payment optimization** – COD and UPI dominated, helping the company streamline payment infrastructure.  
- 👥 **Customer segmentation** – Recognized high-value customers to launch loyalty and retention programs.  
- 📈 **Profit improvement** – Sub-category analysis highlighted profitable areas (Printers, Bookcases) vs. underperformers, enabling corrective action.

---

## 📌 Key Insights
- **Maharashtra (102K) and Madhya Pradesh (87K)** lead in total sales.  
- **Clothing** drives the majority of sales volume (**62% of total quantity**).  
- **Printers (8.6K profit)** and **Bookcases (6.5K profit)** are top-performing sub-categories.  
- Profitability is **highly seasonal**, with strong months in March, October, and November, but losses in July, August, and December.  
- **COD (36%) and UPI (23%)** dominate as preferred payment modes.
